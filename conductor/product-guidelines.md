# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style

For technical documentation (e.g., API references, architectural guides), the prose style should be **Formal and Technical**, emphasizing precision, accuracy, and detailed explanations suitable for developers and advanced users.

For user-facing text (e.g., UI labels, help messages, general marketing content), the style should be **Clear and Concise**, focusing on readability, ease of understanding, and avoiding unnecessary jargon. The tone should be helpful and informative.

## Brand Messaging

The core brand message should revolve around:
*   **Reliability:** Emphasizing the 24/7 uninterrupted streaming capability and automatic recovery.
*   **Efficiency:** Highlighting optimized resource usage and performance, especially with Rust-based transcoding.
*   **Ease of Use:** Stressing the simplicity of setup (Docker Compose) and stream management (Web Admin Panel).
*   **Engagement:** Promoting the ability to foster community interaction through continuous video content.

## Visual Identity

The visual identity should be **Modern, Clean, and Functional**.
*   **Color Palette:** A professional and calm color palette that is easy on the eyes, potentially incorporating shades of blue, grey, and white.
*   **Typography:** Clear, readable sans-serif fonts for both headings and body text to ensure legibility across various devices.
*   **Layout:** Intuitive and uncluttered layouts for the web admin panel, prioritizing functionality and ease of navigation.
*   **Imagery:** Use of clear, relevant icons and minimal, high-quality graphics where necessary, avoiding excessive ornamentation.
